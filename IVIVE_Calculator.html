Equations used

1 · in vitro CLint from t½
CLint [µL/min/mg or µL/min/10⁶ cells] = (0.693 / t½[min]) × 1000 / density
2 · Scale to whole liver
Microsomes: CLint,scaled [mL/min/kg] = CLint × MPPGL × LW / 1000
Hepatocytes: CLint,scaled [mL/min/kg] = CLint × HPGL × LW / 1000
  MPPGL = mg microsomal protein / g liver; HPGL = 10⁶ cells / g liver; LW = g liver / kg body weight
3 · Binding corrections
CLint,u = CLint,scaled / fu,inc   fu,blood = fu,plasma / Rb
4 · Hepatic clearance (RN = fu,b · CLint,u / QH)
Well-stirred:   CLH = QH · RN / (1 + RN)
Parallel-tube:  CLH = QH · (1 − e−RN)
Dispersion:    E = 1 − 4a / [(1+a)²·e(a−1)/2DN − (1−a)²·e−(a+1)/2DN], a = √(1 + 4·RN·DN), DN = 0.17
5 · Extraction and oral F
EH = CLH / QH   F = Fa · Fg · (1 − EH)
CLH,plasma = CLH,blood × Rb
6 · fu,inc estimators (both at the stated protein concentration C, mg/mL)
Halifax–Houston: log(1/fu,1 − 1) = 0.072·logP/D² + 0.067·logP/D − 1.126
Austin: log[(1−fu,1)/fu,1] = 0.56·logP/D − 1.41
Concentration correction: fu,C = 1 / (1 + C·(1 − fu,1)/fu,1)

This tool applies standard, published scaling. It does not apply an empirical scaling factor (e.g. the ~4–5× under-prediction correction sometimes used for human hepatocytes). If your team decides to adopt one, add it as a species row multiplier in the Scaling factors tab rather than adjusting numbers by hand.
